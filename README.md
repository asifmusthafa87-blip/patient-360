# Patient360: Healthcare Analytics and Insights

An interactive Power BI dashboard that turns patient data into meaningful health insights, covering patient demographics, encounters and medical conditions across Kerala.

## Overview
Patient360 analyzes 1,000 patients and 2,954 encounters (Jan 2023 onward) from six cities: Aluva, Kottayam, Thrissur, Kozhikode, Thiruvananthapuram and Kochi. The goal is to help healthcare teams understand who their patients are, how often they visit, and which conditions are most common.

## Key Metrics
- Total Patients: 1,000
- Average Age: 52.39
- Total Encounters: 2,954
- Total Conditions: 1,570

## Dashboard Pages
| Page | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| Patient Details | Gender split, age-group distribution, patients by city |
| Encounter Analysis | Encounter trends over time |
| Health Analysis | Health indicators and patterns |
| Medical Conditions | Most common conditions and their distribution |

## Tools and Skills
- Power BI Desktop

- Patients are spread fairly evenly across the six cities.
